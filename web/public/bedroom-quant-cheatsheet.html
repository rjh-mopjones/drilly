The Retail Reality

  • You lose on speed, data, capital, fees. You win on size (too small to matter to funds), patience (no mandate), and reach (illiquid/neglected corners).
  • ! Latency arb, HFT market-making, news-speed: not your game. Don't try.
  • Realistic: a real retail edge is small, capacity-limited, and decays. Paper-trade months before risking a cent.

Signals (ASCII maths)

SMA cross: fast MA > slow MA -> long
z-score:   z = (price - ma) / sd
           z < -2 buy, z > +2 sell (revert)
RSI:       <30 oversold, >70 overbought
ATR:       avg true range = vol proxy
momentum:  ret_N = price/price[-N] - 1

! Indicator soup = overfit. Fewer, simpler signals generalise better.

Backtesting: Don't Fool Yourself

  • ! Look-ahead bias: using data you wouldn't have had in real time.
  • ! Survivorship: testing only names that still exist.
  • Model fees + slippage + spread, or the result is fiction.
  • Out-of-sample + walk-forward; in-sample results prove nothing.
  • Event-driven > vectorised for realism (fills, partials).

Overfitting Red Flags

  • Equity curve too smooth / Sharpe > 3 on daily data. !
  • Many parameters, each finely tuned.
  • Great in-sample, falls apart out-of-sample.
  • You tried 200 variants and kept the best (multiple-testing).
  • Fix: fewer params, deflated Sharpe, walk-forward, hold-out you never touch.

"If it looks amazing, it's wrong" — assume fake until it survives OOS.

Risk & Sizing (stay alive)

vol target: w = target_vol / asset_vol
Kelly:      f* = edge / odds  (use 1/4 Kelly)
stop:       exit if loss > R per trade
risk/ruin:  small bets survive variance
max DD:     size so worst case != game over

! Position sizing keeps you alive far more than signal quality. Never bet the account.

Fees & Slippage Math

round trip cost = 2*(fee + half_spread) + slippage
edge_after = edge_gross - round_trip_cost
break-even win needs edge > costs
  • Maker (post) rebate/low fee vs taker (cross) high fee — prefer limit orders.
  • ! High-turnover strategies die on costs at retail size. Trade less, hold longer.

Crypto: Funding & Basis

perp funding paid every ~8h:
  fund_pnl = notional * funding_rate
delta-neutral carry:
  long spot + short perp (or vice versa)
  collect funding, hedge price
  • Positive funding -> shorts pay longs (and vice versa).
  • ! Liquidation + leverage kills; keep margin buffer huge.

DEX / MEV Reality

  • AMM LP earns fees but risks impermanent loss.
  • ! On-chain, you are the prey: sandwich/MEV bots front-run naive swaps.
  • Gas + slippage make small on-chain trades uneconomic.

ML: Low Ceiling

  • Signal-to-noise in returns is tiny; leakage is everywhere. !
  • Non-stationary: yesterday's pattern dies.
  • Use ML to combine a few sane features, not to find magic.
  • Simple rules usually beat a fancy model at retail scale.

Bedroom Infra

  • Cheap VPS for an always-on bot; cron for daily strategies.
  • Log everything; alert on errors + fills; monitor open positions.
  • ! Handle reconnects/outages — a dropped socket mid-position is real money.
  • Secrets in env vars, never in code. Keep it boringly reliable.

☐ Paper-Trading Playbook

  • ☐ Pick ONE simple strategy that fits your capital + time.
  • ☐ Backtest honestly: OOS, walk-forward, real costs.
  • ☐ Reject it if it only shines in-sample.
  • ☐ Paper-trade it live for weeks/months; compare to backtest.
  • ☐ Size for survival (vol target, fractional Kelly, stops).
  • ☐ Track every fill; measure slippage vs assumptions.
  • ☐ Expect small, decaying edges — and that most ideas fail.
  • ☐ Keep it educational; never risk money you can't lose.
