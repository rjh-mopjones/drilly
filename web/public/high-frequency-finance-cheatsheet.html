Market Structure

  • Reg NMS / NBBO: best bid/offer across venues; order-protection rule.
  • Maker-taker: post = rebate, take = fee (drives routing). !

  • Fragmentation + co-location.

Limit Order Book

       ASKS (sell)

--------spread--------
101.00  x150   <- best bid

       BIDS (buy)
mid = (bid+ask)/2

Price-time priority (FIFO): best price first, then earliest. A market buy lifts the best ask. Data structure: price levels + FIFO queues (O(1) add/cancel).

Spread & Liquidity

quoted    = ask - bid
effective = 2*|trade_price - mid|
realized  = effective - adverse mid move

3 spread components: order-processing + inventory + adverse-selection. Realized = what the maker actually keeps.

Microstructure Models

Roll:  spread = 2*sqrt(-Cov(dp_t, dp_{t-1}))
Kyle:  dp = lambda * order_flow   (lambda = impact)
Glosten-Milgrom: spread from adverse
       selection alone (informed traders)

Spread exists even with zero costs, purely from information asymmetry. !

Market Making (Avellaneda-Stoikov)

reservation price:
  r = mid - q*gamma*sigma^2*(T-t)   # q=inventory
optimal spread ~
  gamma*sigma^2*(T-t) + (2/gamma)*ln(1+gamma/kappa)
  • Skew quotes to push inventory q → 0.
  • Tension: earn spread vs adverse selection + inventory risk. Widen/pull in fast markets.

Latency: Tick-to-Trade

flowchart LR MD["market data in"] --> FH["feed handler"] FH --> BK["book build"] BK --> ST["strategy"] ST --> RK["risk"] RK --> GW["order out"]
  • Kernel bypass (DPDK/Onload), busy-poll, FPGA wire-to-wire (ns).
  • Microwave > fiber over land; co-location.
  • ! Optimize tail latency (p99) & jitter, not the mean.

Systems Hot Path

  • Lock-free SPSC queues; avoid false sharing (cache lines).
  • NUMA pin, huge pages, warm caches, no alloc/GC on hot path. !
  • Determinism > throughput. (See Concurrency/OS sheets.)

Market Impact

square-root law:  impact ~ Y*sigma*sqrt(Q/V)
Almgren-Chriss:  min E[cost] + risk_av*Var[cost]
  impact (trade slow) vs timing risk (trade fast)

Temporary impact recovers; permanent = info leakage, stays.

Order-Flow Signals

microprice = (bid*ask_size + ask*bid_size)
             / (bid_size + ask_size)
  • Weights toward the side with LESS size → better than mid. !
  • OFI predicts short-term moves; queue position = fill priority + less adverse selection.
  • Alpha decays in micro/ms; must beat fees + spread + impact.
